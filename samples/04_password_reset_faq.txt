Password & Account FAQ
======================

An original sample document bundled with KAI for demonstration.

How do I reset my password?
---------------------------
1. Go to the company sign-in page and choose "Forgot password".
2. Enter your work email. A reset link is sent that is valid for 30 minutes.
3. Follow the link and choose a new password that meets the policy below.

If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check your spam folder, then contact
the IT help desk.

Password policy
---------------
- At least 12 characters, mixing upper and lower case, a number, and a symbol.
- You cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords.
- Passwords expire every 180 days.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA)
---------------------------------
MFA is required for all accounts. During setup, scan the QR code with your
authenticator app. If you lose your device, contact the IT help desk to re-enroll;
they verify your identity before resetting MFA.

Locked out?
-----------
Five failed sign-in attempts lock the account for 15 minutes. A help-desk agent can
unlock it sooner after verifying your identity.
