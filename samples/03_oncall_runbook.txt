On-Call Runbook
===============

This runbook describes how the on-call rotation works and what to do during an
incident. It is an original sample document bundled with KAI for demonstration.

On-call rotation
----------------
- The on-call schedule runs weekly, handing over every Monday at 10:00.
- The primary on-call engineer acknowledges pages within 15 minutes. If the primary
  does not acknowledge within 15 minutes, the alert escalates to the secondary.
- Hand over any open incidents and context in the #ops channel at the end of a shift.

Severity levels
---------------
- SEV1 - a customer-facing outage or data loss. Page immediately, start an incident
  channel, and notify the incident commander.
- SEV2 - degraded service or a failing non-critical component. Respond within 30
  minutes.
- SEV3 - a minor issue with no customer impact. Handle during business hours.

During an incident
------------------
1. Acknowledge the page so the rotation knows it is being handled.
2. Open an incident channel named #inc-YYYYMMDD-short-title and post a short summary.
3. Mitigate first, diagnose second - restore service before root-causing.
4. Keep a timeline of the actions taken in the incident channel.
5. When resolved, post an all-clear and schedule a blameless postmortem within three
   business days.

Escalation contacts
-------------------
- Database issues: the data platform team.
- Networking or VPN issues: the infrastructure team.
- Security incidents: page the security on-call directly and do not discuss details
  in public channels.
