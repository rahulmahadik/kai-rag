VPN & Remote Access Guide
=========================

An original sample document bundled with KAI for demonstration.

Requesting access
-----------------
Remote access to internal systems is granted through the VPN. Request access in the
IT portal under "VPN access"; your manager's approval is required and usually
completes within one business day.

Connecting
----------
1. Install the VPN client from the IT portal.
2. Sign in with your work account and approve the MFA prompt.
3. Once connected, you receive an address on the internal network.

Internal network ranges
-----------------------
Internal services live on private address ranges (for example 10.0.0.0/8 and
192.168.0.0/16). These are not reachable from the public internet, which is why the
VPN is required to reach internal dashboards, databases, and wikis.

Split tunneling
---------------
Only traffic to internal ranges goes through the VPN; normal internet browsing does
not, which keeps the connection fast. Do not disable split tunneling unless the
infrastructure team asks you to.

Troubleshooting
---------------
- "Authentication failed": confirm your password has not expired and approve the MFA
  prompt within 30 seconds.
- "Cannot reach internal host": confirm the VPN shows connected and that you are
  using the internal hostname, not a public one.
